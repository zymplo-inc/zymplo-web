# Security Policy

Zymplo Inc. takes the security of our software seriously.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please email: **security@zymplo.com**

Encrypt sensitive details with our PGP key (fingerprint published at
https://zymplo.com/.well-known/security.txt). We acknowledge reports within
24 hours and aim to provide a patch or mitigation within 7 days.

## Scope

In-scope: production services at *.zymplo.com, all repositories under the
`zymplo-inc` GitHub organization, the Zymplo WhatsApp bot, and Zymplo mobile
applications on iOS and Android.

Out-of-scope: third-party services, social-engineering of employees,
denial-of-service, automated scanner output without exploit demonstration.

## Safe-Harbor

Good-faith research conducted in accordance with this policy is authorized.
We will not pursue legal action against researchers who comply.
